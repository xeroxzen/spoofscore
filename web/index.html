Can someone send email pretending to be your company?

Fake invoices, payment-instruction changes and "urgent request from the CEO" emails work best when they appear to come from your real domain. Check whether yours is protected in a few seconds.

What this checks

DMARC tells receiving mail servers what to do with email that claims to be from your domain but fails authentication. Without an enforcing DMARC policy (p=reject or p=quarantine), forged email is delivered.

SPF lists the servers allowed to send your mail. A mistake here can quietly let anyone pass.

What it doesn't check

DKIM signing keys (they can't be listed without knowing your selector names), lookalike domains, or the security of your mailboxes.

Privacy

Your browser reads public DNS records through Cloudflare's DNS-over-HTTPS service. Nothing is sent to the domain being checked, and no results are stored by this page.
